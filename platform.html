CORE PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Purpose-built around the work of coordinating site services.

The core product scope prioritizes the records, workflows and controls that help construction teams move from a requirement to a managed service and a documented closeout.

01

Customer, project and site management

Maintain organization accounts and multiple projects with registered and trading names, billing and administrative contacts, authorized users, project numbers, clients, contractors, categories, descriptions, status, dates, assigned teams, procurement contacts and related documents. Project stages can distinguish planning, preconstruction, mobilization, active construction, paused, substantially complete and closed. Each project can contain one or more site locations.

  • Site addresses, cities, states, ZIP codes, coordinates where available and delivery entrances
  • Access instructions, operating hours, site contacts, site restrictions, maps and photographs
  • Location-specific service requirements, quantities, specifications, priorities and assigned owners
  • Organization-level separation and role-based access to projects, providers, documents and financial records
02

Service planning and requirements

Define what a project needs before a provider is selected. Each requirement can capture category, description, site, quantity, specifications, start and end dates, priority, responsible employee, preferred provider, estimated cost and supporting records.

  • Configurable categories and service-specific information
  • Requirement states from identified through quotation, approval, order, schedule, active and complete
  • Project and site association for every requirement
  • Clear ownership of outstanding requirements
03

Provider management

Keep existing supplier relationships organized. Provider records can include registered and trading business names, addresses, primary and emergency contacts, telephone and email details, service categories, operating hours, reported geographic coverage (cities, counties, states, ZIP codes or service radius), equipment and capacity, delivery and installation capabilities, servicing options, emergency response information, licensing or qualification details, insurance documents, billing contacts, payment terms, invoice instructions and customer-specific notes.

  • One provider associated with multiple service categories
  • Availability records can identify whether information is customer-entered, provider-confirmed, quote-based, order-confirmed or supplied through an authorized integration; reported coverage is not a confirmed booking
  • Quotation, contract, insurance and communication records
  • Provider activity history and, as the product expands, evidence-based performance reporting
04

Procurement and quotation coordination

Create service requests from a project, site, requirement or active service. Identify potential providers using recorded category, coverage, capability, customer preference and available information.

  • Request quotations from one or more providers
  • Record responses and upload supplier quotations
  • Compare price, scope, quantity, rental duration, delivery, installation, recurring charges, exclusions and terms
  • Track unanswered requests, quote expiration, approval status and quote history while preserving the original quoted terms
  • Create an order from an approved quote or record an agreed service arrangement; retain the provider, project, site, approved amount, service dates, delivery instructions and approval record
05

Delivery, installation and service scheduling

Coordinate the transition from an approved service arrangement to the site. Record requested delivery windows, access details, provider confirmation, site contacts, equipment, installation instructions, completion evidence and unresolved issues.

  • Distinguish requested, proposed, confirmed, rescheduled, completed and cancelled delivery states
  • Record installation dates, providers, site locations, equipment, instructions, site contacts, photographs, completion details and outstanding issues
  • Track active service dates and planned end dates
  • Maintain separate records for completion and any remaining issue
06

Ongoing service operations and change management

Keep service records active after installation. Coordinate recurring servicing, maintenance appointments, additional units, missed collections, equipment replacement, service interruptions and other operational requests.

  • Issue reports with category, priority, location, affected service, attachments and assigned party
  • Quantity, location, delivery and frequency changes; rental extensions and additional capacity
  • Relocation, termination and removal requests, including new location, provider confirmation, transportation needs, additional charges and final documents
  • Provider communication, resolution notes and chronological service history
07

Documents, records and accountability

Associate project and service documents with the correct project, site, provider or requirement, and control access according to organizational roles.

  • Quotations, purchase orders, service and rental agreements
  • Provider insurance and licensing documents where relevant
  • Delivery confirmations, installation records and service reports
  • Invoices, photographs, change approvals and removal confirmations
  • Chronological history of requests, quotes, decisions, changes, communications, service reports and closeout
  • Access controls for viewing, uploading, downloading, reviewing and administering records
08

Cost and project-level financial visibility

Track the commercial record of each service without trying to replace the customer’s accounting system.

  • Quoted, approved and ordered amounts
  • Rental and recurring costs, delivery and installation fees, additional charges and recorded actual costs
  • Quote-to-recorded-cost variance
  • Invoice attachments, due dates, payment status and payment references
  • Project spending views by category, provider, month and site, including approved versus recorded costs, additional charges and outstanding invoices
  • Provider billing details such as billing address, accounts-payable contact, payment terms, tax or vendor identifiers where appropriately collected, and invoice submission instructions
ROLE-BASED PARTICIPATION

The right people see the records and actions relevant to their work.

SITESERV supports internal project and procurement teams while allowing limited participation from external parties where the customer authorizes it.

PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT DIRECTION

Start with dependable coordination. Expand where the work calls for more.

Advanced capabilities follow customer demand, operational readiness and technical feasibility—not a promise that every integration or automation is available from day one.

CORE RELEASE PRIORITIES

Build the complete service workflow.

Organization and project records, site profiles, service requirements, provider details, quote collection, approval status, service orders, delivery and installation scheduling, active service records, requests and changes, document management, notifications, basic project-level cost visibility, notifications and reminders, service history and audit records.

FUTURE EXPANSION

Broader coordination and insight.

Potential extensions include automated quote requests, configurable purchasing approvals and procurement rules, advanced quotation comparisons, repeat-order workflows and purchase-order integrations; supplier onboarding and verification, provider portals and APIs for availability, order confirmations, scheduling and invoice retrieval where authorized; multi-project dashboards, shared provider records, cross-project reporting and consolidated costs; advanced cost and budget analysis, evidence-based provider performance analytics, service utilization and executive reporting; multi-site service calendars, service forecasting, automated reminders, conflict detection, project-phase planning and advanced activity reporting; mobile field operations, additional service categories and selected purchasing or transaction capabilities.

A supplier marketplace is not a prerequisite for SITESERV. Marketplace or transaction functions are evaluated only if validated customer demand and the commercial model support them.

CLEAR PRODUCT BOUNDARIES

Coordination software—not a replacement for every system on the job.

SITESERV organizes the customer’s service requirements and provider relationships. It does not initially own or directly supply temporary infrastructure, operate generators or utility equipment, provide electrical or other engineering advice, certify installation safety, or perform security monitoring unless a later authorized service or integration provides it. It does not replace general construction project management, enterprise procurement, full accounting or ERP systems; guarantee provider availability or performance; execute every supplier payment; or provide a nationwide rental marketplace in its initial release.

Provider availability must be confirmed by the provider or through an authorized integration. A provider match is an administrative recommendation based on recorded information, not a guarantee of capacity, qualification or availability. Initial scope also excludes universal automated supplier bidding, supplier-payment processing, advanced inventory management, replacing construction schedules, advanced AI procurement, complex provider scoring, multi-country operations and extensive custom enterprise integrations. Financial records distinguish a quotation, approved commitment, invoice and recorded payment. Technical work and regulatory or safety determinations remain with the responsible qualified parties.
