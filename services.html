SITESERV organizes records and provider coordination for these categories. It does not itself provide utility service, sanitation, waste collection, installation, security staffing or monitoring, engineering advice or safety certification. Those services remain with the contracted and appropriately qualified providers.

SERVICE-SPECIFIC INFORMATION

Each category carries the details needed to coordinate it.

Power, sanitation, waste, connectivity and temporary facilities do not share the same operating requirements. SITESERV’s configurable structure lets teams record the relevant quantities, specifications, locations, dates, servicing needs, provider capabilities, approvals and documents for each type of service.

The goal is not to force every service into one generic form. It is to keep each service’s important details connected to the project and visible through its lifecycle.
