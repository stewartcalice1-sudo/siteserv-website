From the first requirement to project closeout.

THE SERVICE LIFECYCLE

One connected process, with the project as its context.

The workflow starts before a supplier is selected and continues after a service arrives on site. Each stage carries the details and history needed for the next action.

01
PROJECT SETUP

Create the project and site.

Set up the customer organization, project profile, key dates, assigned personnel and one or more site locations. Record addresses, delivery entrances, operating hours, access instructions, contacts and site-specific restrictions.

02
REQUIREMENTS

Define what the site needs.

Record the service category, description, location, quantity, specifications, required dates, priority, internal owner, preferred provider, estimated cost and supporting documents. Requirements can exist before a provider is chosen.

03
PROVIDER COORDINATION

Identify suitable providers.

Use recorded service categories, reported geographic coverage, capabilities, equipment, customer preferences and existing relationships to help organize provider options. Provider availability remains subject to confirmation.

04
QUOTATION & REVIEW

Collect and compare quotations.

Request pricing from one or more providers, record responses, attach quotations and compare price, scope, quantity, rental duration, delivery, installation, recurring costs, exclusions and terms. Track unanswered requests and quote validity.

05
APPROVAL & ORDER

Record the decision and service commitment.

Assign approvals, preserve the selected quotation, record order and service-agreement details, approved amounts, dates, provider and responsible employees.

06
DELIVERY & INSTALLATION

Coordinate the arrival and setup.

Organize delivery windows, site access details, provider confirmation, equipment requirements, installation instructions, site contacts and completion evidence. Keep unresolved issues visible.

07
ACTIVE OPERATIONS

Manage service, maintenance and change.

Record recurring servicing, maintenance appointments, service requests, missed collections, equipment replacement, additional units, location changes, rental extensions, service issues and provider responses.

08
REMOVAL & CLOSEOUT

Complete the service record.

Coordinate relocation or removal dates, provider confirmation, transport, final charges and closeout documents. Retain the full service history with its requests, quotes, approvals, orders, communications, issues and costs.

VISIBLE STATUS THROUGH THE WORKFLOW

See where each requirement stands—and what comes next.

Service records progress through defined stages. Requested, proposed, confirmed and completed activity remain distinct so teams can see what is known and what still needs attention.

Identified→Awaiting specification→Ready for quotation→Quotation requested→Awaiting approval→Ordered→Scheduled→Active→Completed

Cancelled and other configured statuses are also retained. Active service records distinguish planned, awaiting provider confirmation, scheduled, active, issue open, change requested, removal requested, removed and closed. Schedule records distinguish requested, proposed, confirmed, rescheduled, completed and cancelled delivery activity.
