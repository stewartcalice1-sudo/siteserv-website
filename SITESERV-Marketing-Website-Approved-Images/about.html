THE CUSTOMER REALITY

The site needs many services. The team has to bring them together.

Project personnel balance schedules, subcontractors, budgets and daily site activity while coordinating temporary infrastructure from multiple providers. Each arrangement brings its own contacts, documents, dates and follow-up. SITESERV is designed to make that layer of work easier to organize.

One project. Multiple providers. A changing set of site requirements.

The goal is not to add another disconnected tool. It is to give the people responsible for site services a shared project context for the requirements, decisions and actions they already manage.

A PRACTICAL PRODUCT POSITION

Clear about the work SITESERV does—and the work it does not.

SITESERV is construction-site services coordination software. It is built around organizing the operational process, not claiming responsibility for field services delivered by others.

SITESERV helps organize

Requirements, providers, quotations, approvals, orders, schedules, service changes, documents, cost records and closeout.

Providers remain responsible for delivery

Temporary infrastructure, equipment, installation, maintenance, servicing and removal are performed by the contracted provider.

It works alongside existing systems

SITESERV is not a general construction project-management replacement or a full accounting and ERP system.

Professional responsibility stays clear

SITESERV does not provide engineering advice, certify installation safety or guarantee provider availability or performance.
