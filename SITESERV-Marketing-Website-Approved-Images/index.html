THE WORK BEHIND THE WORK

Before a project can move, the site has to be ready.

A construction site depends on a network of temporary services. Each one can involve a different provider, quote, delivery window, agreement, service schedule and point of contact. The work of coordinating them often falls to the same project team already managing the build.

01 / MANY MOVING PARTS

Every service has its own details.

Specifications, quantities, site access, delivery instructions and service dates are easy to scatter across emails, calls and spreadsheets.

02 / ONE PROJECT

Different providers still serve the same site.

Project teams need a shared view of who is supplying what, what has been approved and what still needs an answer.

03 / THE SITE CHANGES

The coordination continues after delivery.

Service levels, locations, maintenance, collection schedules and removal dates shift as construction progresses.

A MORE CONNECTED WAY TO MANAGE IT

Keep the service details with the project they support.

SITESERV is software for organizing temporary site-service requirements and the work around them. Instead of treating each order as a separate thread, teams can follow the service from the initial need to its closeout.

01

Define the requirement

Record the service, site location, specifications, quantity, timing and responsible team member.

02

Coordinate providers

Keep provider contacts, quotation requests, responses and commercial details connected.

03

Approve and schedule

Record decisions, service arrangements, delivery windows and installation details.

04

Manage through closeout

Track active service, maintenance, changes, issues, documents, costs and removal.
