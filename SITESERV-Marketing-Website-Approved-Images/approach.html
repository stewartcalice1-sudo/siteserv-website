HOW IT WORKS

From the first requirement to project closeout.

A site-service requirement creates a chain of work: define what is needed, coordinate the provider, review the terms, schedule the arrival, manage the active service and close it out. SITESERV keeps that sequence connected.

THE SERVICE LIFECYCLE

One connected record as the work moves forward.

The project is the context. Each requirement carries its own provider, details, decisions and activity, while the team retains a view of the services supporting the site.

01
PROJECT & SITE

Start with the place the service supports.

Set up the project and site location, key dates, assigned personnel, contacts, delivery entrances, operating hours, access instructions and site-specific restrictions.

02
REQUIREMENT

Describe what the site needs.

Record the service category, description, location, quantity, specifications, required dates, priority, internal owner, preferred provider and supporting information.

03
PROVIDER COORDINATION

Bring the right provider details into view.

Organize existing provider relationships by service category, recorded coverage, capabilities, customer preferences and available information. Provider availability remains subject to confirmation.

04
QUOTATION & REVIEW

Understand the proposed service before committing.

Record quote requests and responses. Review price, scope, quantity, rental duration, delivery, installation, recurring charges, exclusions and terms, while keeping unanswered requests visible.

05
APPROVAL & ORDER

Keep the decision and commitment together.

Record the selected quotation, approval status, service arrangement, provider, approved amount, dates and responsible employees.

06
DELIVERY & INSTALLATION

Coordinate the arrival at the site.

Organize delivery windows, site access, provider confirmation, installation details, site contacts and completion evidence. Keep unresolved issues visible.

07
ACTIVE SERVICE

Manage what happens after setup.

Record recurring servicing, maintenance appointments, service requests, missed collections, equipment replacement, additional units, location changes, rental extensions and provider responses.

08
REMOVAL & CLOSEOUT

Close the loop and retain the history.

Coordinate relocation or removal dates, provider confirmation, transport, final charges and closeout documents. Keep the service history with its requests, quotations, approvals, orders, communications and changes.

STATUS THAT MEANS SOMETHING

See what is confirmed, what is active and what still needs attention.

Requested, proposed, confirmed and completed activity should not be confused. SITESERV is designed to keep the recorded status and next action clear as each requirement progresses.

Requirement identified→Quotation requested→Under review→Approved→Ordered→Scheduled→Active→Closed

The workflow can also retain cancelled requests, provider responses, rescheduled deliveries, open issues, requested changes and removal activity. A recorded status does not replace provider confirmation.
