SERVICES & INFRASTRUCTURE

The essential services that keep a site operational.

Temporary site infrastructure is not one purchase or one provider. SITESERV helps construction teams organize the different service requirements around the project, site, provider and schedule.

SITE SERVICE CATEGORIES

Different services. Different details. One project context.

Each category has its own specifications, delivery and installation requirements, service frequency, provider responsibilities and removal needs. SITESERV keeps those details organized without forcing every service into the same process.

01

Temporary power & generation

Temporary electrical service, generators, power distribution equipment, temporary panels, connection and fuel arrangements, installation requirements and removal needs.

02

Water supply & connections

Temporary water supply, connection requirements, storage tanks, water delivery, pumping equipment, supply schedules and service changes.

03

Internet & site communications

Temporary broadband, wireless and cellular connectivity, network equipment, installation appointments, activation, equipment return and removal.

04

Sanitation & hygiene

Portable restrooms, handwashing stations, temporary sanitation facilities, unit quantities, placement, cleaning schedules, servicing frequency and removal.

05

Waste management & recycling

Dumpsters, roll-off containers, waste collection, recycling containers, construction debris removal, pickup schedules, exchanges and overflow requests.

06

Fencing & barriers

Temporary perimeter fencing, pedestrian and traffic barriers, access gates, temporary partitions, installation, layout changes and removal.

07

Security & access systems

Temporary surveillance equipment, security cameras, access-control systems, alarm services, monitoring providers, installation and servicing arrangements.

08

Temporary lighting

Construction and perimeter lighting, portable lighting towers, emergency lighting equipment, installation and maintenance schedules.

09

Temporary offices & facilities

Site offices, portable buildings, storage containers, worker welfare and break facilities, meeting spaces, delivery, installation, relocation and removal.

10

Equipment & site support

Generators, temporary pumps, compressors, lighting equipment, temporary heating or cooling and selected site-support equipment.

SITESERV is software for organizing service requirements and provider coordination. It does not itself supply utilities, sanitation, waste collection, installation, security staffing, monitoring or equipment. Those services are performed by the contracted providers.

THE DETAILS THAT MATTER

Keep service-specific information attached to the requirement.

A temporary power requirement is not the same as a waste collection schedule. SITESERV is designed to capture the details that matter to each category while keeping the information connected to the same project and site.
