THE CUSTOMER PROBLEM

The site needs many services. The team needs a way to coordinate them together.

General contractors and developers manage temporary infrastructure while balancing schedules, subcontractors, budgets and day-to-day site activity. Each service can involve a different provider, agreement, delivery arrangement, servicing obligation and closeout process.

SITESERV focuses on that layer of work. It organizes service requirements and provider coordination around the project, helping teams move beyond scattered emails, calls, spreadsheets and separate records.

WHO BENEFITS FROM THE WORKSPACE

A PRACTICAL PRODUCT POSITION

Manage the services your business already relies on.

SITESERV is vendor-neutral. It supports the customer’s existing provider relationships and does not depend on a supplier marketplace or requiring every provider to become a full platform user.

Work with current providers

Maintain provider records and customer-specific information across existing relationships.

Keep responsibility visible

Connect internal owners, provider contacts, site locations and service requirements.

Keep the full record

Retain quotations, approvals, service changes, documents, costs and closeout history.

Expand when the need is proven

Advanced automation, integrations, multi-project reporting and additional capabilities follow validated customer demand and technical readiness.

WHAT SITESERV IS—AND IS NOT

A coordination platform with a defined job.

Clear boundaries help customers understand where SITESERV fits alongside their providers, project systems and professional responsibilities.

SITESERV coordinates

Requirements, providers, quotations, approvals, orders, schedules, service changes, documents, cost records and closeout.

Providers deliver services

Temporary infrastructure, equipment, installation, maintenance and other work remain with the contracted provider.

Project systems retain their roles

SITESERV is not a general construction project-management replacement or a full accounting/ERP system.

Qualified parties retain technical responsibility

SITESERV does not provide engineering advice or certify installation safety, and it does not guarantee provider availability or performance.
